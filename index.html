Privacy Policy

D³ · Android application: com.auzogames.doodoodaze
Last updated: November 11, 2026

Auzo Games provides D³ — Doo Doo Daze. This policy explains information handled by the current Android game, its Google advertising and consent services, this policy website, and email support.

At a glance: You can play without creating an account. Game progress is saved on your device. The Android test build includes Google AdMob demonstration banners and Google's consent service. Google may process information when these services connect. Contact us at d3doodoodaze@gmail.com.

1. Game progress and preferences

The game stores progress, scores, earned credits, character ownership, equipped characters, rotation choices, reward timing, and settings on your device. Music playback state may also be stored locally for continuity. Auzo Games does not operate a player-account service or a gameplay cloud-save server in this build, and the game does not upload these saves to an Auzo Games server.

The Android application permits operating-system backup. Depending on your device and backup settings, Android or your device provider may back up and restore eligible app data. Those services follow their providers' policies and your device settings.

2. Google advertising and consent services

The Android build integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The current test build requests Google demonstration banners only; live advertising and rewarded video ads are not enabled. Test ads do not mean that Google's services make no network requests or process no information.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK can collect or share the following types of information, depending on SDK configuration, consent, device settings, and features in use:

  • IP address, which can indicate approximate location.
  • Device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID where available.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches and taps.
  • Technical and diagnostic information about app or SDK performance.

Google processes SDK information for purposes including ad delivery, measurement and analytics, security, and fraud prevention. The game requests non-personalized test banners. Non-personalized advertising can still involve technical identifiers, local storage, and data processing.

UMP contacts Google to determine applicable consent requirements and retrieve consent messages. It processes consent choices and associated technical information and stores relevant preferences on your device. The game checks consent status before requesting an ad. If that check fails, gameplay remains available without the banner.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps. Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures explain its data categories in more detail.

3. Your choices

When required and available, the game displays Google's consent form. An Ad privacy choices control appears in Settings when Google's service requires access to privacy options. You can revisit those options and change your choices. Availability and the choices shown depend on your location and Google's applicable configuration.

You can also use Android's advertising privacy controls to reset or delete your advertising ID where supported. Android's backup controls govern operating-system backup. Sound, music, vibration, and reduced-motion preferences are available in game Settings where supported.

4. Information you send us

If you email d3doodoodaze@gmail.com, we receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request, troubleshoot problems, and maintain support records. Our email provider, Google Gmail, processes those messages under its applicable terms and privacy policy. Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

5. Sharing and service providers

The game does not provide an Auzo Games feature to upload or sell your local gameplay save. Information processed by Google advertising and consent services, email support, operating-system backup, and this website's hosting is handled by the providers described in this policy. We may also disclose support information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or protect legal rights and security.

6. Retention and deletion

Local progress generally remains until you clear the app's storage or remove the app. Uninstalling generally removes the local copy, but operating-system backups may restore eligible data if backup remains enabled. Clearing storage can permanently remove game progress; Auzo Games cannot recover a save it does not hold.

We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to respond to requests, resolve issues, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. You may ask us to delete support correspondence using the contact address below. Google's services and your device's backup provider apply their own retention and deletion policies.

7. Teens and children

Doo Doo Daze is intended for teens aged 13 and older and adults. It is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to Auzo Games, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action, including deleting information we hold where required. Applicable consent ages may differ by location.

8. Privacy requests and security

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection, or to withdraw consent. Contact us about information held by Auzo Games. For information held by Google or a device-backup provider, use that provider's privacy controls and request channels. We may need appropriate information to verify and respond to your request, but will not ask you to send a password.

No electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We limit our direct collection to the information described above. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own under their applicable policies.

9. This policy website

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitor IP addresses for security purposes. This page adds no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies, tracking pixels, or contact forms. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. Updates and contact

We will update this page when our data practices change and revise the date above. This policy describes the current Android test build; future services will require corresponding disclosures before they are enabled.
